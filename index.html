Freelance Service Agreement

InkClause · Contract #FA-2026

This Agreement is entered into between Jane Smith ("Freelancer") and Acme Corp ("Client") for the purpose of Web Design services.

1. Scope of Work

The Freelancer agrees to deliver: Design a 5-page marketing website with custom illustrations. Any work outside this scope requires a written change order and additional fees.

2. Payment Terms

Total fee: $2,500. A 50% deposit is due before work begins. The remainder is due upon delivery. Late payments incur a 2% monthly fee.

3. Timeline

Final delivery by Dec 31, 2026. Delays caused by Client extend the deadline accordingly.

4. Ownership

All deliverables remain property of the Freelancer until full payment is received.
